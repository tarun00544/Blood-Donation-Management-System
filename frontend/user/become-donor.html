💡 Blood Donation Guidelines

  • Donors must weigh at least 50 kg and feel healthy on the day of donation.
  • A minimum interval of 90 days (3 months) is required between consecutive blood donations.
  • Ensure you stay hydrated and have eaten a light meal before giving blood.
